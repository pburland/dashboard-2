# Nutrition digest (from "Recomp and fueling plan", Oct 2026)

Plan of record for food. Numbers for a 72 kg athlete with ~58.3 kg fat-free mass. The full report is kept outside the repo.

## Bottom line
- 150 lb at 12% needs about -13 lb fat and +3 lb lean during an Ironman build: possible but a stretch. Realistic landing 13-15% (150 lb at ~14% if lean mass holds).
- Working RMR 1,850 kcal (midpoint of the mono-time 1,958 measurement and the predicted 1,748) until a healthy re-test (Dec/Jan). Daily life ~ RMR x 1.45 (~2,650) plus training.
- Deficit only on rest, lift and easy weekdays, never on key or long days or weekends: maintenance to ~Nov 29 (mono return); -400 to -550 kcal in base blocks Nov 30-Dec 20, Dec 28-Jan 17, Jan 25-Feb 14 (a maintenance week between); -200 to -300 Feb 15-28; maintenance Mar 1-21 (70.3 taper, race, recovery); -200 to -350 in Build 2 blocks Mar 22-May 23; maintenance through peak, taper and race week.
- Rate: 0.4-0.6% bodyweight a week in base, <=0.3-0.4% in build, never over 0.7% (Garthe 2011).
- Energy availability (intake minus training, per kg fat-free mass): >=30 on deficit days (~1,750 kcal left after training), 40-45 at maintenance, never plan below 25 (IOC REDs 2023). Main risk: underestimating big days, not the planned deficit.

## Daily targets
- Protein 160 g every day (2.2 g/kg; Morton 2018, Helms 2014): 4 x 35-40 g plus ~40 g casein-rich food before bed. 175 g if lean mass slips.
- Carbs by day (g/kg): rest 3-4, lift 3.5-4, easy 4-5, key 5-7, long run 6-8, long ride 8-10. No deliberate train-low.
- Fat >=65 g and >=20% of energy.
- In-session: under 60 min none; 1-2.5 h 30-60 g/h; over 2.5 h 60-90 g/h glucose:fructose; Ironman bike 80-90 g/h. Gut training at race rates from January (70.3) and mid-April (Lake Placid) (Costa 2017).
- Recovery: next session within 8 h -> 1.0-1.2 g/kg/h carbs for 4 h. Evening sessions: dinner with 30-40 g protein, then ~40 g casein.

## Weekly correction rules (7-day weight averages)
R1 no change in weeks 1-2 of a block. R2/R3 off target by >0.15 kg/wk for 2 weeks -> +/-150-200 kcal/day (cuts only from rest/easy days, only if every day still clears EA 30). R4 loss >0.7%/wk -> add back now. R5 maintenance drift >0.5 kg over 2 weeks -> +/-150-200. R6 any day below EA 30 (deficit) or 25 -> raise it. R7 two or more REDs signs (fatigue, low libido, poor sleep, falling performance, low testosterone/T3, illness) -> maintenance >=2 weeks and see a doctor. R8 suspend in race weeks, carb loads, travel, heat blocks. R9 bench or squat trend falling 2+ weeks in a deficit -> halve the deficit. R10 new RMR -> rescale.

## Supplements, labs, race rules
- Creatine 3-5 g/day (8 g is more than needed); no changes in race week. No caffeine before evening sessions; race ~3 mg/kg pre-start, Ironman total <=~6 mg/kg.
- Labs Nov, Jan, May-Jun: ferritin (+CRP), iron saturation, vitamin D, testosterone, free T3. Iron only if ferritin <35; vitamin D only if <30 ng/mL.
- Fluid and sodium from a sweat test; heat starting point 500-900 mL/h and 500-1,000 mg sodium/h; drink to thirst, never finish heavier than the start (hyponatremia).
- Race week: carb-load 10-12 g/kg for 36-48 h, low fiber and fat; race morning 2-3 g/kg 3-4 h before. Mono: no near-max breath-holding lifts until mid/late November.
