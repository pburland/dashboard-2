# Roadmap digest: to 70.3 Puerto Rico (Mar 14 2027), then Lake Placid (Jul 25 2027)

Agreed Nov 5 2026 after his doctor cleared him (spleen normal). Goal for both races: finish strong. Both judged
realistic: he has finished a 70.3 (Eagleman, 7:19), has ~16 weeks of full training after the Japan trip, and the
70.3 leads into a 19-week Ironman build. Main risk: post-viral fatigue, so build gradually and use the checkpoints.

Weekly targets by two-week block (easy unless noted; lifting on top):
- Nov 9-22 (Japan): run only 10 -> 14 mi (long 5); bodyweight strength. ~2.5 h.
- Nov 23-Dec 6: swim 2x (~3,500 yd); bike 2 rides 2.5 h (long 1:30); run 14-16 mi (long 6). Tests: run LTHR Dec 1, swim CSS Dec 7. ~6-7 h.
- Dec 7-20: swim ~4,500 yd; bike 3.5 h (long 2:00); run 16-18 (long 7); strides; bike FTP test Dec 16. ~7-8 h.
- Dec 21-Jan 3: swim ~5,000; bike 4 h (long 2:15); run 18-20 (long 8); lighter holiday week. ~8 h.
- Jan 4-17: swim ~6,000; bike 4.5-5 h (long 2:30, 2 x 10 min moderate); run 20-22 (long 9); 80/20. ~9 h.
- Jan 18-31: swim ~6,500; bike 5 h (long 2:45-3:00); run 20-24 (long 10); first 15-min brick. ~9-10 h.
- Feb 1-14: swim ~7,000 with CSS sets; bike 5.5-6 h (long 3:00-3:15 with race-effort blocks) + one interval ride; run 22-25 (long 11) + tempo 3-4 mi; 20-30 min bricks. ~10-11 h.
- Feb 15-28: swim ~7,500; bike 6 h (long 3:30 partly at 70.3 effort); run 24-26 (long 12 = peak) + 30-40 min brick; heat practice; race fueling on long rides. ~11 h.
- Mar 1-14: taper (~70% then ~40%); last heavy legs Thu Mar 4; race Mar 14.

Checkpoints (the weekly report checks them; app/checkpoints.py):
- Dec 20: a ~7-8 h week handled with normal resting HR and sleep.
- Jan 31 go/no-go: a 3 h ride, a 9-10 mi run, ~2,000 yd continuous swim, resting HR/readiness normal, easy-run pace at
  the same HR improving. If missed: switch to 70.3 Texas (Apr 4, Galveston, flat) or Oceanside (Apr 3, cold water,
  hilly) for three more weeks; check Puerto Rico's transfer policy.
- Feb 21: a 3.5 h ride plus a 30-min brick, OK the next day.
- Any time: unusual fatigue for more than a week -> back off and tell his doctor.

Heart rate: pre-mono easy runs averaged ~146 at ~10:20/mi (summer); since Oct 19, ~145 at ~11:40/mi (about 10% less
efficient): lost fitness, not illness. Easy-run cap is 145 average until the Dec 1 LTHR test sets zones; brief
climbs to 155-160 on hills are fine; minutes above 160 on a flat easy run mean slow down.

Garmin: from Nov 23 2026 the app puts each planned run, ride and swim on his Garmin calendar automatically
(after the morning sync and Sunday's plan). The Japan trip runs (Nov 11-20) were added by hand.
